# ANALYSIS PLAN, ROUND 45 (follow-up of simulated review round 1; written BEFORE running; 2026-10-24)

Source: the simulated-review report of round 1 (2026-10-24), concerns R2.M3 and R2.M5. No model training; only stored out-of-fold predictions. CPU only. Two small analyses; both are reported as they are, including if negative or not supporting the manuscript.

## Analysis A (R2.M3): F2-Clean vs F2 within year x device strata with an age caliper
Data: `hasil44/bruto_strata.npz` (keys `point`, `boot`, `names`, `ks`): AUC within strata per k (5, 10, 15, infinity) for 12 scores (mean of 10 repetitions), with 2,000 patient-bootstrap resamples shared by all scores (`round44_ringkas.py`: resampling within the strata cells, pairs rebuilt per k). Quantities: the AUC difference within strata (F2-Clean minus F2-adj) and (rank-normal I2 without age, D2, minus I2-adj) at k = 5, 10, 15; six tests in total. Difference per resample = difference of the columns on the same resample; CI = 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles; two-sided bootstrap p = 2 x min(proportion <= 0, proportion >= 0) with a floor of 1/2,000; Holm over six tests. I2-adj is taken from `hasil29/B_prediksi_data_asli.npz` (V4_AS) if it is not in `bruto_strata.npz`: if the I2-adj column is not available on the same resamples, the second contrast is recomputed with a patient-level bootstrap with fixed seed 45001 using the pair function of `round41_strata_kaliper.py` and reported with that label.
Predictions (written before seeing the differences; point estimates from `ringkas_strata.csv`: F2-Clean 0.7727 vs F2-adj 0.7299 at k = 10): (P1) the point difference F2-Clean minus F2-adj is positive at k = 5, 10, and 15 (90%); (P2) the 95% CI of the difference at k = 10 does not include zero before Holm (50%); (P3) after Holm over six tests no difference is significant at alpha 0.05 (60%); (P4) the I2 contrast is similar to F2 (same direction; 85%). Reporting criterion: the difference is reported as limited evidence if Holm >= 0.05, whatever the point estimate; the manuscript must not state that F2-Clean "retains" or is "better" without exceeding this criterion.

## Analysis B (R2.M5): inference sensitivity, patient-level bootstrap
Data: `hasil30/eval_prediksi_v4.npz` (keys F2_adj, C1, Floor, CBraMod_FT) and I2-adj from `hasil29/B_prediksi_data_asli.npz` (V4_AS). Statistic: pooled AUC per repetition (200 patients) averaged over 10 repetitions. Class-stratified patient bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, seed 45002. Four differences: F2-adj minus I2-adj, minus C1, minus Floor, minus CBraMod_FT. Percentile CI; two-sided bootstrap p (floor 1/2,000); Holm over four tests. Compared with the per-fold Nadeau-Bengio t-test (test table of the manuscript).
Predictions: (P5) all bootstrap CIs of the four differences include zero (80%); (P6) the conclusion "not significantly different" does not change (same direction and Holm < 0.05 status as Nadeau-Bengio) in all four differences (80%); (P7) the width of the bootstrap CI for the difference is narrower than the per-fold Nadeau-Bengio CI in >= 3 of 4 differences (60%). Criterion for "conclusion changes": if the bootstrap Holm < 0.05 while the Nadeau-Bengio p >= 0.05 or the reverse. If it changes, this is reported in Results and Methods and the related claim is softened.

## What will be reported even if negative
All differences and CIs of both analyses; predictions that missed; deviations are recorded as dated addenda in this file.
