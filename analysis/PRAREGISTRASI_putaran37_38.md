# Analysis plan, rounds 36c, 37, and 38: paired leakage test for adjustment on log features (2026-10-22)

## Basis (round 36 results already seen)
In Rohira (RF on windows), V4 leaks on raw features with all ways of fitting the coefficients (win 0.573/0.562; pat 0.575/0.563; win cross-fit 0.566/0.563; pat cross-fit 0.564/0.563; H1/H2a). Logistic regression on patient means does not leak (0.51-0.52). The raw features have very heavy tails (median kurtosis 8,481 on windows and 110 on patient means; Tang-F per second 17,301, values up to +-8e8), whereas the log features are close to Gaussian (2.96; Tang symlog -1.6; NM token 114-d 0.8). On log features, RF with adjustment: logpat 0.523/0.538, logwin 0.530/0.537, LR-logpat 0.503 (H1). Hypotheses H_A (weighting) and H_B (in-sample residual shift) are rejected; H_C (heavy tails + nonlinear classifier) is supported. In H2a, RF without adjustment is already at 0.540 because that scenario keeps the era signal in the features and linear regression cannot remove a nonlinear era signal, so the absolute rule R1 is not suitable for H2a with nonlinear classifiers.
## New rule R2 (paired; fixed before the paired computation and the additional replicates)
For every adjusted pipeline and scenario (H1, H2a), the paired difference delta_r = AUC(with adjustment) - AUC(without adjustment) on identical permuted data (same seed) in each replicate r; the comparator without adjustment uses the same pipeline (Rohira: RF without adjustment; Tang: symlog without adjustment; NM: standard normalization without covariate regression). Decision: no evidence of leakage if the mean delta <= 0.01 AND the mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.03; leakage if the mean delta > 0.03; otherwise small use/uncertain. The absolute rule R1 is still reported on H1 (mean <= 0.52 and mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.54). A pipeline is declared leak-free only if R2 is met on H1 and H2a and R1 on H1. Note: the unpaired mean AUC values of RF-logpat (0.523, 0.538) and RF-logwin were seen before this rule was written; the paired delta was not.
## Round 36c (CPU, Rohira): `round36c_paired.py`
RF without adjustment (raw features; RF is invariant to monotone transforms) and RF-logpat (patient-mean coefficients unweighted, log features, all training patients) on 30 replicates (seed 33000 + r; the first 10 identical to round 36) for H1 and H2a. If R2 is met: the real data Rohira-logV4p and Rohira-logV4p+AS (age/sex as per-window columns) are reported against Rohira-F and Rohira+AS (Nadeau-Bengio difference).
## Round 37 (GPU, Tang-F): `round37_tang_log.py`
Per-second features are passed through symlog (sign(x) log1p(|x|); fixed, data-independent constant). Variants L (without adjustment; paired comparator), Lp (coefficients from the per-patient mean, all training patients including validation), Lw (optional). The reduced null test (repetition 0, 5 folds, H1 and H2a, 20 replicates, seeds 34000 + rep and 34300 + rep) is run for L and Lp before the real data; the real data (50 folds, 199 patients) for L and Lp are run, but the Lp result is reported as leak-free only if R2 and R1 are met.
## Round 38 (GPU, NM): `round38_nm_paired.py`
NM with a fixed configuration as in the round 30 null test (d = 32, L = 32, one seed, maximum 20 epochs, without age/sex), variants N0 (standard normalization without covariate regression; paired comparator) and V4 (regression on the segments of all training patients), H1 (seed 30500 + rep; the first 20 replicates identical to round 30) and H2a (permutation within era; seed 30900 + rep), 20 replicates. If V4 fails R2: NM with per-patient coefficients (mean of segments per patient, unweighted) becomes a candidate, tested with the same rule before the real data.
## Predictions (before the paired results)
- Rohira-logpat: delta H1 <= 0.01 (50%); delta H2a <= 0.01 (65%); R2 met on both (35%).
- Tang-Lp: R2 met on H1 and H2a (45%); R1 H1 <= 0.52 (55%).
- NM-V4: delta H1 <= 0.01 (55%) and R2 met on H1 and H2a (45%).
- Real data (if the null passes): |Rohira-logV4p+AS - Rohira+AS| < 0.05 and not significant (65%); Tang-Lp - Tang-L not significant (65%); Tang-L (symlog without adjustment) higher than Tang-F 56.2 (60%).
## Note
The design was written after seeing the round 36 results (raw and log cells); the choice of symlog and rule R2 was guided by the null test and feature-distribution diagnostics, not by the real-data AUC (the real-data AUC of Rohira-V4 had already been seen from round 33). No claim of superiority comes from this round.
