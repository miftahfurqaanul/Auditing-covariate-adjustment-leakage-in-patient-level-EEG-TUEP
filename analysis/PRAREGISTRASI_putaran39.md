# Analysis plan, round 39: leakage map of confound adjustment on clustered data (simulation with zero signal) (2026-10-22)

## Aim and gap
To map when regression-based confound adjustment leaks label information into the classifier on clustered signal data (windows within patients), with an empirical anchor from the EEG results of rounds 28-38. Hamdan et al. 2023 (card 21) studied i.i.d. tabular data (mechanism: non-normal features + nonlinear models; the risk rises with the number of features). Not yet mapped here: clustered data, aggregation per patient, imbalance in the number of windows, classifier capacity (including LSTM), and the operating characteristics of the paired null test R2. The mechanism is NOT claimed as a new discovery.
## Data generator (round39_simulasi_kebocoran.py; zero disease signal)
n patients (default 200), m windows per patient (default 50), p features (default 50); x_pj = mu_p + e_pj with mu_p ~ N(0, I) and the distribution of e: normal, lognormal (X = exp(mu_p + e)), Student t df 3, contamination (1 percent of windows multiplied by 100), standardized chi-square df 3. The label is balanced and does NOT depend on the features. The covariates mimic TUEP: era with 5 categories with P(era | label) from the cohort table (interpolated with strength s: s = 1 as TUEP, s = 0 no label-covariate association), age with a 0.45 SD difference (epilepsy younger, multiplied by s), sex independent; design D = [1, age z, sex, 4 era dummies] (7 columns, same as I2). Scenario H1: the features do not depend on the covariates; H2a: mu_p plus a covariate effect (coefficients N(0, 0.3^2)) so that a legitimate era signal remains in the features. Windows per patient uniform or unbalanced (m_p = m exp(0.6 N(0,1)) clipped to [m/4, 4m], ratio about 16 as in Rohira).
## Pipeline
Transforms are fitted only on training patients: raw; symlog (sign(x) log1p(|x|)); rank (inverse-normal from the training quantiles; 1,001-point quantiles). Adjustment: none; win (window-weighted OLS on the training windows); pat (OLS on the per-patient mean, unweighted). Adjusted features = T - D_p B. Classifiers: CPU: LRpat (patient mean + nested_lr: StandardScaler + LogisticRegressionCV 9 values of C, 5 folds), RF (100 trees), ET (ExtraTrees 100 trees), the last two on windows with the score averaged per patient; GPU: XGB (100 trees, device cuda), MLP (2 layers of 64), bidirectional LSTM + attention (sequence of 30 windows per patient). Evaluation: 5 stratified folds per patient, one repetition, pooled AUC over n patients (one prediction per patient). All configurations on the same data and folds; paired difference delta = AUC(transform, adjustment) - AUC(same transform, none). Tree classifiers are invariant to monotone transforms, so the baseline none is computed once (raw). A replicate generates data from the seed = crc32(cell parameters + replicate index), so the data are identical across the CPU and GPU chains. Age/sex are not classifier inputs.
## Decision rules (same as rounds 37-38, not changed)
R2: per cell and configuration, mean delta <= 0.01 and mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.03 = no evidence of leakage; mean > 0.03 = leakage; otherwise small use/uncertain. R1 (absolute, H1): mean AUC <= 0.52 and mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.54.
## Controls
Negative: s = 0 (no label-covariate association; leakage is impossible by construction) and normal i.i.d.: mean delta <= 0.01. Positive: chi-square or contamination with RF and pat adjustment at s = 1, mimicking Hamdan et al.: delta > 0.03 (expected). If the positive control is not detected in the pilot, the design is considered insensitive and is revised through an addendum before stage B.
## Stages
A (pilot): 20 replicates per cell; cells: 5 distributions x {H1, H2a} at n = 200, m = 50, p = 50, s = 1, plus the s = 0 control (normal and chi-square, H1); CPU chain (LRpat, RF, ET; 8 configurations) and GPU chain (XGB, MLP, LSTM; H1 only; 4 configurations: raw none, raw pat, symlog none, symlog pat). B (main; at least 100 replicates per cell; neural cells at least 50): extended on the meaningful cells to n (100, 200, 500), m (10, 50, 200), p (10, 50, 200, 1000), imbalance, s (0.5, 1), and transforms, with a budget of about 40 CPU hours and about 20 GPU hours; trimmed cells are recorded. C: empirical anchor from the existing EEG results (without retraining). D: operating characteristics of R2 (power and type I error on 10-50 replicates with an injected true delta of 0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.05) from the stage B replicate deltas.
## Predictions (subjective probabilities; written before the full pilot)
- Negative control mean delta <= 0.01 for all classifiers (95%); the positive control is detected (RF on chi-square or contamination with pat or win, delta > 0.03; 70%).
- LRpat does not leak in all distributions and adjustments (85%).
- RF/ET leak (delta > 0.03) on contamination and lognormal without transform (75%); do not leak on normal (85%); t3 and chi2 leak mildly (delta 0.01-0.03; 50%).
- symlog or rank lowers delta on lognormal and contamination to <= 0.01 (70%).
- win and pat do not differ meaningfully (|difference of delta| < 0.01; 70%).
- XGB leaks like RF (70%); MLP and LSTM leak less than RF (55%).
- On H2a the RF delta is not clearly larger than on H1 (60%).
## Note
A dry run (one replicate per classifier for timing and a function check) was seen before this document was finalized; no delta summary of the pilot was seen. Design changes after the pilot are written as dated addenda. Negative results are reported. Simulation is not clinical data; the distributions covered are limited.

## Addendum 1 (written AFTER seeing the stage A pilot summary; BEFORE stage B; 2026-10-22)
Pilot result (ringkasan_pilot.csv; 20 replicates per cell): the negative control passed (s = 0 and normal features: delta <= +0.009 for all classifiers); the positive control was detected (raw lognormal, H1, s = 1: RF +0.164, XGB +0.157, ET +0.146, MLP +0.096, LSTM +0.022; contamination: RF +0.061, ET +0.069, XGB +0.060, MLP +0.057, LSTM +0.047); per-patient logistic regression does not leak in any distribution; chi-square df 3 and t3 (small sample kurtosis) do not leak; rank-INT lowers delta to <= +0.010 in all cells; symlog (constant 1) only partly (RF lognormal +0.102; contamination +0.017 to +0.027). Because the windows per patient are uniform in the pilot, the win and pat adjustments are mathematically identical (window-weighted OLS = patient-mean OLS if m_p is equal); the difference can only appear with window imbalance.
Observation about the criteria: on H2a, the baseline without adjustment contains a legitimate era signal (AUC 0.58-0.64) so the paired delta is strongly negative (the adjustment removes it) and R2 gives the decision "no evidence" although the AUC of the adjusted features is still above chance (for example raw lognormal RF 0.638; contamination ET 0.547). Additional rule for H2a (fixed before stage B): the H2a decision uses the absolute AUC of the adjusted features against 0.5 (leakage if the mean > 0.55; no evidence if the mean <= 0.52 and mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.54; otherwise small/uncertain), reported alongside R2; in the simulation the era effect is linear so the adjustment should remove it completely, whereas in real data a nonlinear era signal can remain (Rohira log H2a 0.538 in RF without adjustment 0.540).
Design changes for stage B (from the pilot): (i) a kurtosis continuum so that the map includes the EEG points (Rohira window kurtosis 8,481; Tang 17,301): lognormal X = exp(sig (mu + e)) with sig in {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0}; contamination with (fraction, scale) in {(0.003, 100), (0.01, 10), (0.01, 100), (0.03, 100), (0.01, 1000)}; (ii) additional transforms: symlogc (sign(x) log1p(|x| / c) with c = median |x| per feature from the training windows) and log (positive data only; oracle for lognormal); (iii) window imbalance (win differs from pat only if unbalanced; win is computed only in cells with imbalance); (iv) one-factor-at-a-time variation around the base cell (n = 200, m = 50, p = 50, s = 1, H1) for lognormal (sig = 1) and contamination (0.01, 100): n in {100, 500}, m in {10, 200}, p in {10, 200}, imbalance, s = 0.5, s = 0 (control), and the kurtosis continuum; H2a only on the base cell and the kurtosis continuum points (CPU only); (v) replicates: CPU at least 100 per cell, GPU 50 per cell (H1 only). p = 1000 was dropped from stage B for the time budget (recorded as a trimmed cell).
Additional predictions (before stage B): leakage rises monotonically with kurtosis and reaches a plateau at kurtosis above about 100 (65%); rank-INT and log lower delta to <= +0.01 across the whole continuum for RF (70%); symlogc is better than symlog but not as good as rank-INT (60%); delta falls with n (65%) and rises with p (65%); delta does not depend meaningfully on m (55%); win is larger than pat under imbalance (50%); the EEG points (kurtosis 10^3-10^4, RF/LSTM) fall in the leakage region of the map (70%).
Budget trimming of stage B (written before stage B was run): ExtraTrees dropped from stage B (equivalent to RF in the pilot; CPU chain: per-patient logistic regression and RF); heavy cells (m = 200, p = 200, n = 500) use 50 replicates (CPU); neural cells (GPU chain: XGB, MLP, LSTM; H1 only) 30 replicates, below the minimum of 50 in the instructions and recorded as trimming; other CPU cells 100 replicates.

## Addendum 2 (written AFTER stage B and the independent check; BEFORE the follow-up; 2026-10-23)
The independent check found that the pre-registered negative-control criterion (delta <= 0.01) was violated by two PILOT cells (20 replicates): normal features, s = 1 (the label is related to the covariates but the features are related to nothing), symlog transform, pat adjustment, RF delta +0.021 (SE 0.008) and XGB +0.026 (SE 0.008). The normal s = 1 cell with raw and rank transforms, and all s = 0 cells (86 control rows; 97.7 percent with the decision "no evidence"), passed. A possible explanation (not yet tested): noise (86 rows, two cells passing 2.6-3 SE) or a small real effect of the symlog kink that makes normal features slightly non-normal.
Follow-up (stage C, fixed before the run): normal, t3, and chi-square cells with H1, s = 1, n = 200, m = 50, p = 50 with 100 replicates (CPU: LRpat and RF; GPU: XGB, MLP, LSTM 30 replicates), all stage B configurations (raw, symlog, symlogc, rank; none and pat). Decision: if the mean symlog delta on normal for RF and XGB is <= 0.01 with 100 replicates, the violation is declared pilot noise; if > 0.01, it is declared a small effect of symlog on a normal distribution (symlog must not be used without a null test) and reported in the manuscript. The negative-control criterion is not loosened.
