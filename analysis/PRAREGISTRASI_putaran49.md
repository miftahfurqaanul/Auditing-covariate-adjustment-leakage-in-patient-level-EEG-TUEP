## 49A. Strict nesting (M3; sensitivity analysis)

Code-audit finding: in I2, P3, C1, and the metadata baseline, `evalkit.nested_lr` = StandardScaler + LogisticRegressionCV, so the scaling, the V4/V5 adjustment, the mean reference covariance of P3, and the PCA are fitted on all 160 outer training patients before the inner CV chooses C; the inner validation patients take part in the label-free transforms (the outer test patients never do). NM-V4 fits the V4 coefficients (segment-weighted) and the standardization of the age/sex summary on all training patients, including the 20% validation patients used to choose the configuration and for early stopping.

Design: every data-dependent transform is refitted only on the training data of each inner fold.
- I2, C1, metadata: for each outer fold, StratifiedKFold(5) without shuffling over the outer training patients in index order (as in LogisticRegressionCV); in each inner fold: V4 (OLS [1, age, sex, era] on the inner training patients; divided by the SD of the inner training residuals), add age and sex, inner-training StandardScaler, L2 LogisticRegression (lbfgs, balanced class weights, max_iter 5000) for nine values of C 10^-3..10^1; best C = highest mean inner AUC; then the whole chain is refitted on the 160 outer training patients with that C and predicts the 40 test patients. C1 and metadata: without V4.
- P3 (V5): global covariance reference (mean of the training-window covariances) -> tangent space -> patient mean -> PCA 50 -> V5 -> + age/sex -> scaler -> LR; all refitted in every inner fold.
- NM (V4): the V4 coefficients and the standardization of the summary are fitted only on the training patients (without the 20% validation); the validation is still used to choose the configuration (d, L) and for early stopping (best-epoch model, without retraining); five seeds are averaged. Seeds, grid, and validation split are the same as in round 30.
- F2-strict = mean probability of I2, P3, NM strict; F2-strict without NM = mean of I2 and P3 strict.
- Checks before the run (displaying no strict-version AUC): the "loose" mode (transforms fitted on the outer training set) of the new code reproduces the stored predictions I2_adj, C1, Floor (`hasil30/eval_prediksi_v4.npz`) within 5e-3 per patient on the first 5 folds; loose P3 reproduces P3_adj on the first fold; loose NM reproduces the stored test AUC of fold r0k0. Indices: no outer test patient in any fitting set (assertion), no inner validation patient in a transform fit (assertion).
- Measure: per-fold AUC (mean of 50) and pooled; difference strict - original per model with a Nadeau--Bengio CI. No family test; descriptive.
- Reporting rule (fixed now): the main numbers of the manuscript are NOT replaced. If |per-fold difference F2-strict - F2| > 0.010 or the decision "not shown to exceed metadata/C1" changes, this is stated in Results and Discussion (not only in the supplement).
- Prediction: |difference| <= 0.010 for I2, C1, metadata (75%); for P3 (60%); for NM (50%); F2-strict remains not significantly different from metadata and C1 (90%).

## 49B. Operating characteristics of rules R1/R2 (M6)

Aim: the frequency of the decisions "leakage / no evidence / uncertain" when leakage is absent (null controls) and when it is present with a known size (positive controls), the sensitivity to the number of replicates and to the thresholds, and the separation of Monte Carlo error from patient variation.
- Stored replicate pools (not retrained). Null controls (without adjustment; features without meaning): C1 of round 29 (H1, 100), Rohira RF-none (round 36c, 30), Tang L (round 37, 20), NM-N0 (round 38, 20), plasmode cells without adjustment (round 42; RF, XGB, Tang, LR; gamma 0/0.5/1), simulation cells s = 0 and cells "none" (round 39). Positive controls with a leak known by design: V0 of round 29 (100 replicates; reference size = mean of 100 replicates), plasmode cells raw-win/raw-pat RF gamma = 1, raw lognormal simulation cells RF/XGB (reference delta = mean over all replicates of the cell). R2 pairs from the same paired replicates (V0-C1 and V4-C1 of round 29; RF-logpat - RF-none; Tang Lp - L; NM V4 - N0; simulation cells pat - none).
- Empirical frequencies: n in {10, 20, 30, 50, 100} (not exceeding the pool size) drawn with replacement, 10,000 draws; 95% Monte Carlo CI from an outer bootstrap of the pool (500).
- Parametric OC curves: replicate AUC ~ Normal(mu, sigma^2), sigma = the SD of the pool (minimum, median, maximum among the null controls); mu 0.48--0.62 (step 0.005); R2: delta ~ Normal(d, sigma_d^2), d 0--0.06. 20,000 simulations per point.
- Threshold sensitivity (frozen now; the manuscript decisions still use the original thresholds): R1-strict (<= 0.51 and bound <= 0.53; leakage > 0.54), R1-original (0.52/0.54; > 0.55), R1-loose (0.53/0.55; > 0.56); R2-strict (0.005/0.02; leakage > 0.02), R2-original (0.010/0.030; > 0.030), R2-loose (0.015/0.040; > 0.040). Reported: the number of published decisions (leakage table, S2, S3, plasmode, round 40 simulation map) that change.
- Monte Carlo error vs patient variation: C1 and V4 (I2 without age/sex) H1 rerun with 100 replicates (seed of round 29) saving the out-of-fold predictions; the patient-bootstrap SD (2,000; class-stratified; fixed predictions) of the mean replicate AUC compared with the Monte Carlo SE = SD of replicates / sqrt(n). Check: the replicate AUC equals `hasil29/A_kebocoran_replikasi.csv` within 5e-3.
- Prediction: on null controls, P(R1 "leakage") <= 0.05 for n >= 20 in all pools except pools with a mean below 0.5 (70%); P(R1 "no evidence") >= 0.80 at n = 100 for C1 (75%); for V0 (mu ~ 0.56), P(R1 "leakage") >= 0.80 for n >= 30 (65%); R2 on null controls P("leakage") <= 0.05 (80%); the patient-variation SD > 3 x the Monte Carlo SE at n = 100 (85%); at least one published decision changes under the strict or loose thresholds (80%).

## 49D. Isolating the window structure within patients (M1)

Question: with features without disease signal (H1), do the intra-patient correlation (ICC), the number of windows per patient (m), and the order of aggregation relative to adjustment change the leakage caused by covariate adjustment, with the marginal distribution and the number of patients fixed?
- Generator (derived from `round39_simulasi_kebocoran.make_data`): n = 200 patients (100/100), covariates as TUEP (age, sex, era; label-covariate strength s = 1), p = 20 features, latent z = sqrt(ICC) u_patient + sqrt(1 - ICC) e_window (N(0,1) marginal for all ICC), then the marginal distribution: normal (z), lognormal exp(z), contamination (z with 1% of windows multiplied by 100). The features do not depend on the label or the covariates. Factors: ICC in {0, 0.3, 0.7, 0.95}; m in {10, 50, 200}.
- Arms (all on the same data and folds; one repetition of 5 stratified folds per replicate): window RF without adjustment; A = window RF + per-window adjustment (OLS on the training-window rows); B = window RF + per-patient adjustment (OLS on the training patient means, subtracted from the windows); patient RF without adjustment (patient-mean features); C = aggregate first (patient mean) then patient-level adjustment, patient RF; patient LR (patient mean, LogisticRegressionCV) without adjustment, with per-window adjustment, and per-patient. RF: 100 trees, balanced class weight; patient score = mean window probability.
- Measure: delta = pooled AUC (arm with adjustment) - pooled AUC (control without adjustment at the same classifier level), per replicate. Replicates: 20 per cell (set from a target precision SE of delta <= 0.006 if the SD of delta <= 0.027; the SD of delta of the lognormal RF cell of the round 39 pilot was about 0.02--0.03). No additional replicates after seeing the results.
- Primary family (Holm, two tests; lognormal distribution, m = 50): (D1) delta A at ICC 0.95 - delta A at ICC 0 (Welch t-test, one-sided > 0); (D2) delta A - delta C at ICC 0 (paired t-test over replicates, one-sided > 0). Others descriptive.
- Prediction: D1 positive and passes Holm (55%); D2 positive and passes Holm (65%); the difference A - C narrows at ICC 0.95 compared with ICC 0 (55%); LR |delta| <= 0.01 in all cells (80%); normal distribution |delta| <= 0.01 for all arms (75%); delta A rises with m at ICC 0 (40%; no strong direction).
- Claim: if D1/D2 do not pass, the repeated structure is NOT claimed as a novelty; the result is still reported.
