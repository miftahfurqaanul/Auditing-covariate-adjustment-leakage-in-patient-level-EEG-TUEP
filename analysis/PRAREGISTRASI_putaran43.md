## 1. Question
(P7) Does the gap of the frozen foundation models (BENDR/LaBraM) to I2 in rounds 31-32 come from unequal pipelines? The pipelines are equalized: PCA 50 components -> rank-normal -> V4 adjustment -> L2 logistic regression with 6 values of C chosen by inner CV, plus frozen CBraMod. (P2) Does anatomical compression (19 channels -> 5 regions x 5 bands, median + IQR across windows = 50 features) raise the AUC with a larger EPV (80 training events/50 features = 1.6 vs 0.86 for I2)? (P6) How much selection optimism (model selection after 42 rounds on the same 200 patients) can be estimated from the stored out-of-fold prediction matrices, and does the best model still exceed metadata and C1 after correcting for selection?

## 2. Parts A-B: equal pipelines on the 50 canonical folds
**Protocol:** 50 canonical folds (`A.J`), per-fold AUC (mean; Nadeau-Bengio CI) primary, pooled AUC secondary; all fitting only on the 160 training patients of each outer fold.
**Patient-level inputs:** (i) BP = `A.BP` (114 features); (ii) LaBraM = mean of the LaBraM window embeddings (200-d) over the first-session windows; (iii) CBraMod = mean of the CBraMod window embeddings of layer 12 (200-d) [descriptive: layers 4 and 8]; (iv) BENDR = mean of the window embeddings (512-d); (v) Comp50: for each window, the mean of the `rel_{band}_{channel}` features (5 bands; stored values) over the channels in a region -- frontal {FP1, FP2, F7, F3, FZ, F4, F8}, central {C3, CZ, C4}, temporal {T3, T4, T5, T6}, parietal {P3, PZ, P4}, occipital {O1, O2} -- then the median and IQR across the first-session windows per patient = 5 regions x 5 bands x 2 = 50 features; Comp25 (median only) descriptive. `logtot` is not used.
**Pipeline (equal):** [PCA] -> rank-normal -> V4 -> [+ standardized age, sex] -> L2 LR (`class_weight=balanced`), C chosen from {1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1, 1, 10, 100} by 5-fold stratified inner CV on the training patients (AUC; ties -> smaller C); all steps (standardization + PCA 50, 1,001-point rank quantiles, V4 OLS on D = [intercept, age, sex, 4 era indicators], residual divided by the training SD) are REFITTED in every inner fold; the final model is fitted on all training patients. PCA only for BP-eq, LaBraM, CBraMod, BENDR (dimension >= 114); Comp50 and BP-rank without PCA.
**Models:** BP-eq (114 -> PCA50), BP-rank (114, without PCA), LaBraM-eq, CBraMod-eq (L12; L4, L8 descriptive), BENDR-eq, Comp50, Comp25; each with and without age/sex (+AS and noAS). Main = +AS (comparable to I2-adj V4+AS 68.0).
**Primary family (Holm, 4 tests, two-sided, Nadeau-Bengio per-fold AUC difference):** LaBraM-eq(+AS) - I2-adj; CBraMod-eq(+AS) - I2-adj; BENDR-eq(+AS) - I2-adj; Comp50(+AS) - I2-adj. I2-adj = `hasil29/B_prediksi_data_asli.npz` V4_AS.
**Descriptive (no claim, no correction):** difference from C1, metadata, F2-adj; noAS variants; BP-rank and BP-eq vs I2-adj (effect of rank/PCA); CBraMod layers; Comp25.

## 3. Part C: leakage test of the equal pipelines (R1)
Permuted-feature test with the REAL label, WITHOUT age/sex (because AS legitimately carries label information): H1 (global exchange of patient rows) and H2a (within era), 20 replicates per condition, seed 43000+rep, noAS pipeline for BP-eq, LaBraM-eq, CBraMod-eq, BENDR-eq, Comp50, using the five outer folds of the first repetition (`A.J[:5]`). R1 on the mean per-fold AUC: mean <= 0.52 and mean + 1.645 SE <= 0.54 = "no evidence of leakage"; > 0.55 = "leakage"; otherwise "uncertain". (A paired R2 comparator is not needed: there is no equivalent "without V4" pipeline; only R1 is reported.)

## 4. Part D: selection-optimism correction
**Candidate prediction matrices (10 x 200, out-of-fold, 50 canonical folds):** S_utama = {I2-adj, P3-adj, NM-adj, F2-adj, C1, LaBraM-FT, CBraMod-FT, BENDR V4+AS (hasil31), LaBraM V4+AS (hasil31), DNM-V4 (hasil33), BP V4 tuned (hasil32), LaBraM-eq, CBraMod-eq, BENDR-eq, Comp50 (round 43, +AS)}. S_semua = all stored matrices in hasil29-hasil33, hasil30 (`eval_prediksi_v4.npz`) and round 43, EXCEPT (a) pipelines known to leak (V0, V1, V2, V3 in hasil29; F2_lama, I2, P3, NM without -adj in hasil30; Rohira_V4*, Rohira_F? remains (without adjustment) included), (b) non-EEG comparators (Floor, Demo), (c) identical duplicates. Reference: Floor (metadata) and C1.
**Metric:** candidate AUC = mean over the 10 repetitions of the AUC over the selected patients (the same as the pooled AUC of the project). Winner = the candidate with the highest AUC.
**Optimism correction (bootstrap 2,000, seed 43100):** per replicate: draw patients with replacement, class-stratified (100/100); in-bag = the sample, OOB = patients not drawn (both classes required); winner_b = argmax in-bag AUC; optimism_b = in-bag AUC(winner_b) - OOB AUC(winner_b). Optimism = mean; corrected AUC of the winner = full AUC of the winner - optimism (and a percentile CI of the corrected AUC is not reported; the distribution of the optimism is reported). The rank of F2-adj and the corrected AUC of F2-adj with the same optimism are also reported (F2 was chosen as the proposed method).
**Max-T test (Reality Check/Westfall-Young, 2,000 bootstraps, seed 43101):** H0: no candidate is better than the reference R (R = Floor or C1; candidate C1 is excluded when R = C1). d_k = AUC_k - AUC_R; t_k = d_k/SE_boot_k; T_obs = max_k t_k; T*_b = max_k (d*_k,b - d_k)/SE_boot_k; p = mean(T*_b >= T_obs). Two tests (R = Floor, R = C1) on S_utama and on S_semua; Holm over the two references within each set.
**Limits:** this correction applies only to selection among candidates whose predictions are stored; it does not cover the degrees of freedom of candidate design (features, architecture) or the alignment before round 28; the predictions themselves are already out-of-fold (no inner selection leakage).
