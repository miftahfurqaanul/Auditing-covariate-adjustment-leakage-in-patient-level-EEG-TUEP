# Analysis plan, round 32 (tier 2) and 33 (tier 4) (2026-10-21)

Aim: to correct two inequalities in the comparison (see the answer to the owner): (32) unequal tuning budgets between our pipeline and the comparators; (33) published comparators without covariate adjustment. CPU only. Patients and the 50 canonical folds are the same as in the previous rounds (already used for 30 rounds; every result here remains exploratory-confirmed, not independent confirmation). Tang-F (neural network, GPU), LaBraM-FT, CBraMod-FT, and NM/P3 are NOT recomputed here (they remain without adjustment/equal budget; stated as a limitation).

## Round 32: `round32_anggaran_setara.py`
Patient-level feature sources: BP (band power 114-d; with V4 = I2-adj, without = C1), frozen BENDR (8,704-d -> StandardScaler + PCA 128 inside training), frozen LaBraM (200-d), DNM (57-d). Adjustment: none or V4 (OLS on all training patients). Age/sex are always appended -> 8 pipelines.
The search space is IDENTICAL for all pipelines (6 configurations): LR-L2 (C chosen by inner CV; default), PCA16/32/64 + LR-L2, LR-L1 (C in {0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1}, CV 5), RF (300 trees, minimum leaf 3). Selection: mean AUC of 5-fold stratified inner CV on the training patients; ties -> the order above. V4 adjustment and PCA are refitted inside every inner fold. The "default" result (LR-L2 only) is reported alongside.
Primary tests (Holm, 4 tests; per-fold AUC, Nadeau-Bengio t-test): BP|V4|tuned (I2-adj) minus BP|none|tuned (C1), BENDR|V4|tuned, LaBraM|V4|tuned, DNM|V4|tuned. Descriptive (BH): effect of V4 adjustment - none per source; tuning effect (tuned - default) per pipeline; I2-adj tuned vs the existing I2-adj, LaBraM-FT, metadata baseline. AUC within year x device strata.
Mandatory reproduction (assert): default configuration BP|V4 = I2-adj and BP|none = C1 (max per-fold AUC difference < 5e-3); DNM|none|default = DNM-F of round7 (< 5e-3). Perturbation checks (test X does not change selection/inner scores; test labels change nothing) at the start of the run.
Predictions: (a) reproduction passes (90%); (b) tuning raises the per-fold AUC by < 0.02 for all BP pipelines (70%) and may lower some pipelines (60%); (c) I2-adj tuned does not differ significantly from C1 tuned (80%), BENDR|V4 tuned, LaBraM|V4 tuned, and DNM|V4 tuned (each Holm >= 0.05; 70%); (d) V4 - none is not significant for all sources (75%); (e) the rank of I2-adj among the pipelines is not changed by tuning (60%).
Note: the RF interface uses age/sex per patient as columns; with n = 160 patients RF can exploit patient identity through these features (a property of the pipeline, not train-test leakage).

## Round 33: `round33_pembanding_v4.py`
DNM: patient-level XD (57-d) -> V4 (OLS on all training patients) + age/sex -> nested_lr (DNM-F of round7 + V4). Rohira (set A, 8 channels x 6 bands = 48 features per 10 s window, 28,982 windows): RF 100 trees max_depth 1000, class_weight balanced, trained on 80% of the training patients (split_val as in round7), patient score = mean of the window probabilities. Variants: Rohira-F (reproduction), Rohira+AS (age/sex as per-window columns), Rohira-V4 (OLS of 48 features on the window-owner design, fitted on all windows of training patients), Rohira-V4+AS.
Primary tests (Holm, 4 tests): DNM-V4 - DNM-F; Rohira-V4+AS - Rohira+AS; I2-adj - DNM-V4; I2-adj - Rohira-V4+AS. Descriptive (BH): Rohira-V4 - Rohira-F; Rohira+AS - Rohira-F; DNM-V4 and Rohira-V4+AS vs C1 and the metadata baseline; I2-adj vs DNM-F and Rohira-F.
Leakage test (features without information, without age/sex): DNM patient features permuted between patients (H1 global, H2a within era), 20 replicates; Rohira window sets permuted between patients (the new owner carries its own label/covariates/era), 10 replicates; raw and V4 variants. Round 30 rule: mean pooled AUC <= 0.52 no evidence of leakage, > 0.55 leakage (limited power because of few replicates).
Mandatory reproduction: DNM-F and Rohira-F equal round7 (max per-fold AUC difference < 5e-3). Tang-F is not included.
Predictions: (a) reproduction passes (90%); (b) V4 null <= 0.52 for DNM (70%) and Rohira (60%; a nonlinear RF on windows with per-era estimation error can leak like P3-V4); (c) DNM-V4 - DNM-F: |difference| < 0.04 and not significant (70%); (d) Rohira-V4+AS - Rohira+AS not significant (70%); (e) I2-adj - DNM-V4 and I2-adj - Rohira-V4+AS positive but Holm >= 0.05 (60%).
