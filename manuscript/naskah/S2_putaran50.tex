\subsection*{S2.16 Round 50: replicate extension and an independent plasmode}
Round 50 was planned after a second review (plan hashed before the runs, \texttt{6e4e37e4920c}; the summary script was hashed before its output was seen). One correction followed after the Emotiv output was seen: two print lines of the summary script used a pandas method name as a column name and printed empty lists; the computed tables did not change, and the corrected script was hashed again.

\textbf{Replicate extension (50A).} The extended runs reproduced the stored replicate 0 of both classifiers (largest AUC difference $5.6\times10^{-17}$) and added 90 (\textit{random forest}) and 100 (XGBoost) replicates per cell. In the four null cells ($\gamma=0$, raw features, window-weighted or per-patient adjustment), the mean difference over 120 replicates was +1.0 and +1.1 pp for the \textit{random forest} and +0.9 and +1.0 pp for XGBoost (95\% CIs within $-$0.4 to +2.5 pp; SD 7.5--8.0 pp), against +2.2 to +3.0 pp in the first 30 or 20 replicates; the pool decision R2 was ``uncertain'' for the \textit{random forest} and ``no evidence'' for XGBoost, and R1 was ``no evidence'' in all four. With draws of 20, 50, and 100 replicates from these pools, R2 declared leakage in 10.7\%--13.9\%, 2.7\%--4.2\%, and 0.3\%--0.8\% and ``no evidence'' in 28.1\%--33.4\%, 45.6\%--51.4\%, and 45.6\%--52.1\% (Monte Carlo 95\% CI of the leakage frequency with 50 replicates up to 32.5\%); under a normal model with the pool SD and a true difference of zero, ``no evidence'' would be reached in 80.7\%--82.8\% with 50 replicates. The positive cells ($\gamma=1$, raw per-patient adjustment) gave +11.1 (\textit{random forest}) and +11.6 pp (XGBoost) and were declared leakage in 100\% of draws of 20. Descriptively and outside the plan, rank-normal with per-patient adjustment at $\gamma=1$ gave +0.9 and +0.8 pp over 120 replicates, both ``no evidence'' under R1 and R2 (with 20 replicates, XGBoost had been R1 ``uncertain'').

\textbf{Plasmode on the Emotiv cohort (50B).} Of 308 subjects with clean 10 s windows, 299 had at least five (5,987 windows; 5--27 per subject, median 21; 163 with epilepsy; 206 from Nigeria). The design had five columns: intercept, country, eyes condition recorded first, recording day within the country, and time of day (the last two standardized). The covariates gave an AUC of 68.5 for the real label, and the synthetic labels reached 49.6, 60.1, and 69.1 at $\gamma = 0$, 0.5, and 1 (mean over 100 replicates). \tabref{tab:s2_emotiv} gives every pipeline. Window-weighted and per-patient adjustment leaked alike (\textit{random forest} +12.8 and +12.7 pp; XGBoost +11.3 and +11.6 pp at $\gamma=1$), and the leak grew with $\gamma$ (\textit{random forest} raw adjustment +3.9 pp at $\gamma=0.5$). Symlog and log reduced the leak less than rank-normal (+2.2 to +2.8 pp, all R2 ``uncertain''). In the secondary family, raw minus rank-normal was +11.3 pp (95\% CI +9.1 to +13.6) for the \textit{random forest} and +10.5 pp (+7.8 to +13.3) for XGBoost (Holm $<0.001$). At $\gamma=0$ every adjusted cell had the R1 and R2 decision ``no evidence''.

\textbf{Confidence interval of the Kural demographic baseline (addendum 2).} After the third automated review, which noted that the demographic baseline row of \tabref{tab:transfer} had no CI, the stored prediction was recomputed with the same procedure (age and sex; $10\times5$ cross-validation within the 99 Kural subjects; the AUC equalled the stored value, difference below $10^{-9}$) and a subject-level \textit{bootstrap} gave an AUC of 63.4 with a 95\% CI of 52.7--73.8 (2,000 replicates, seed 20261103). This is descriptive and outside the plan; the script was hashed before it ran, no prediction was written beforehand, and no other number changed. This row is not a transfer of the TUEP model.

\textbf{Predictions of round 50.} Of seven predictions written before computation, six were met: R2 declared leakage in at most 5\% of draws of 50 replicates in all four null cells and in fewer than 31.5\% of draws of 20 in at least three of them (65\% and 60\%; all four), the positive cells were detected (85\%), raw adjustment leaked in both tree classifiers of the Emotiv cohort with Holm $<0.05$ (65\%), rank-normal reduced that leak by at least 3 pp (60\%), logistic regression did not leak (80\%), and the control at $\gamma=0$ passed (85\%). The prediction that all four null means would lie within 1.0 pp of zero with CIs including zero (70\%) was missed: the CIs included zero, but the \textit{random forest} means were 1.05 and 1.09 pp. Seven independent check groups passed (reproduction of stored replicates, second implementation of the summaries and of Holm, band power by a separate Welch implementation, covariates rebuilt from the original metadata files, the round-39 window-count cells from raw replicates, and the decision frequencies with another random stream).
