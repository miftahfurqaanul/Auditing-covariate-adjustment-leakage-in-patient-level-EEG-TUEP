\caption{Audit workflow with the training--test boundaries. (a) Preprocessing and feature extraction are label-free and per recording. (b) Steps that estimate statistics from several patients (steps 1--2) are fitted inside the outer training fold, labels enter only in steps 3--4, and the outer test fold is never used for fitting or selection. (c) The audit layer evaluates the same pipeline, splits, and predictions under null experiments, against non-EEG baselines, within matched strata, and after selection over stored candidates. V4 and V5 are covariate adjustments; H1 and H2a permute the EEG between patients or within era; R1 and R2 are the decision rules; N0--N2 are the models of Subsection~\ref{sec:validasi}.}\label{fig:alur}
